# Footage tags, batch B (printers, sauna, gym, grass)

Method: 1 fps contact sheets plus about 25 extra frames, and 0.5 s RMS loudness on the original audio. Audio cannot be transcribed, so "speech-like" is a guess from level changes. All clips are 720x1280 portrait. Subject positions are as a fraction of frame height. A 16:9 crop of a portrait frame keeps only the middle ~32% of the height at full width, so "16:9 ok?" means a slice of that height holds the subject.

Script shot numbers: 4 grass scrolling, 11 printer, 15 sauna, 16 gym bike, 24 grass ending, 6 glasses on.

Loudness key: dB is RMS per 0.5 s. Room tone is about -50. Noisy outdoor is -15 to -30.
