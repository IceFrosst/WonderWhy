WhyLens · Curio · Unseen

## Punchline
Look at anything. See the physics inside it.

## Description
You are surrounded by physics you can't see: why a guitar string sounds higher when you press it, how a kettle boils, what bike gears actually do. Today you would have to stop, pull out a phone and read a wall of text.

With Meta Ray-Ban Display glasses, you just look at the object and pinch your fingers or ask "why?". A short answer appears in your view, followed by a glowing animation that shows the hidden mechanism at work: the wave on the string, the heat moving through the water, the force through the gears.

Curious for more? Ask a follow-up and go deeper. Say "done" and it disappears.

- **Hands-free and in the moment:** learn while your hands are on the object.
- **Shows, doesn't just tell:** Meta AI talks, we show. Visual explanations work for people who struggle with long text or audio.
- **Any language**, starting at high-school level and getting deeper as you dig.
- **Private by design:** the camera takes one photo only when you ask. Nothing is stored.

Built for students and anyone who wants to understand the world a little better every day.
