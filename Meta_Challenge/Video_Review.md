## 3. Storytelling
- **Arc:** wonder, numb, wonder back, pass it on. It is sound.
- **Hole in the bookend:** the child's grass question is never answered. If a grass object is cheap to add (draft 4 had it), a 2 s answer before shot 24 is the strongest possible ending.
- **Colour:** it works muted, but the answer should trigger it, not the hardware. Product restoring wonder is the whole claim.
- **Sound:** the muffled-to-open motif is invisible muted, so treat it as a bonus layer only. The click on every pinch and on the end card is a good sonic logo.
- **Real proof is thin:** about 22 of 82 s is real display footage, and Video.md asks for at least 60% real demo. The split-screen fix raises it.
- **How it works** earns 3-4 s, not 8. **The share scene** earns about 12 s, not 28.

## 5. Feasibility risks
1. **Daylight.** It is 14:41 and Espoo sunset is around 15:45 (from memory, check it). Sunrise tomorrow is after the 08:00 edit lock, so shot 4 and the gold bookend must be filmed in the next hour or not at all.
2. **"Record display" may not capture what you need.** The phone app shows still cards; live animations are only in the Web App, and its photo route is untested on glasses. Test one existing object now (10 minutes). It decides whether the GLASS shots exist.
3. **Five new objects by 18:00, and the follow-up bug, sit on the same team.** Set a hard gate at 18:00: earbuds are the must-have, and everything else falls back to the seven objects that work.
4. **A second pair needs its own band, phone, pairing and app.** The one-pair rewrite removes this.
5. **On-set audio in the crowd will be bad.** Record all lines as voiceover in a quiet room.
6. **The edit budget is 3 h on Sunday** for 25 shots, composites, sound design and AI generation. Cutting the composites and uploading the safety version tonight protects it.
7. **Sauna:** fogged lens and other people in frame.
8. **Slips in the docs:**

   - The line list omits shot 18's line.
   - Submission.md still says "Physics Lens", "max 2:00" and "never run on real glasses".

**The three changes that help most**
1. **Real answer by 0:10, shown big.** Reorder Act 3 (earbuds out, glasses on, question, real recording in split screen) and let gold arrive with the answer.
2. **Halve Act 7.** One pair, the "How does it know I pinched?" question, real stranger reactions. Spend the saved seconds on the trust beat, the number and the call to action.
3. **Shoot only what is true today.** Use existing objects for the montage, fix "anything", caption every spoken question, and make the "wait cut" tag legible.
