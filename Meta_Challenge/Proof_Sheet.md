# Proof sheet for the peer-review table

`Proof_Sheet.html` is a one-page A4 sheet (what Physics Lens is, three real stills, what is tested, built or planned, honest limits) for reviewers who may not wear the glasses.
To print: open it in Chrome, Print, paper A4, margins default, headers and footers off, background graphics on. It must come out as exactly one page.
Humans must fill in: the QR box ("QR: TODO (human)", 30 mm) with the demo URL, and the team line ("TODO (human)"). Check the demo URL is up before printing.
All facts and labels are copied from the claims table in `Submission.md` and use only the stills under `app/`; the images load by relative path, so keep the file in `Meta_Challenge/`.
Re-check every row after the real-glasses test: "Built, not on glasses" and the limits lines will change, and "nothing has run on real glasses" may stop being true.
